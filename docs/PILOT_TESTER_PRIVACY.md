# SeshLogic pilot — what we store (private tester note)

This is for invited testers only. It is not a public privacy policy.

**Product:** SeshLogic (closed pilot)  
**Contact:** Ellie

## What you create

When you sign in we store:

- Your email (account)
- Gym profiles and equipment
- Preferences and presets (goal + sport)
- Workout history, saved workouts, and week plans

Guest / unsigned-in use is not part of this pilot. You must have an account.

## What we do not do in this pilot

- No public app-store listing
- No ads, no sale of your data
- No requirement to use your real name

## How to delete

In the app: **Profile → Delete account**. That removes the account and synced gyms, presets, and history. It cannot be undone.

If something looks stuck, email Ellie and we will delete the account from the dashboard.

## How we use this

Feedback (screenshots, sport/goal, what felt wrong) is for fixing the pilot. We will not share your workouts or email outside the people running the test.
